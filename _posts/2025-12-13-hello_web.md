This site was created in a little over 2 weeks (nights and weekends, of course), using **Google's Antigravity IDE**.

I wanted to create a space to post articles and get creative. I started looking into **GitHub Pages** and getting a site deployed was **super easy**. I needed theming, but the *Jekyll* defaults did not quite fit my needs.

The next day I downloaded Antigravity, and within an hour I had a custom Jekyll theme that was easily customizable and extendable. Antigravity has **supercharged** my **productivity** and **creativity**. 

This is the first time I've seen a proper **Implementation Plan**, **Task List**, and **Walkthrough**. by an AI Agent. The ability to influence the **Implementation Plan** with in-line comments before the task executes is **brilliant**. And the pending comments let me change directions on the fly, saving reverts and churn.

The **Browser Manipulation** is interesting and will be useful when it works. At this stage it's still a **WIP**, as it didn't recognize missing CSS being applied and generally took a *long time*. I ended up relying on manual verification and found that uploading screenshots with `divs` selected in **Browser Dev Tools** was effective for resolving issues quickly.

I was able to add feature after feature, including porting several features started in **AI Studio** and polished with **opus-4.5** in **Antigravity**. Linking folders using **Workspaces** worked well for porting data and copy to build this site. I mainly used the **Agent Manager** and mostly used one Agent Session at a time.

In the *"Imitation is the sincerest form of flattery"* category, I'm extremely impressed by the UX treatment on [antigravity.google](https://antigravity.google/).

So far, **Antigravity** has been the coolest IDE and most productive AI tool I've used—and the **ROI** is [off the charts](https://antigravity.google/pricing).
