This site was created in a little over 2 weeks (nights and weekends, of course), using **Google's Antigravity IDE**.

I wanted to create a space to post articles and get creative. I started looking into **GitHub Pages** and getting a site deployed was **super easy**. I needed theming, but the *Jekyll* defaults did not quite fit my needs.

The next day I downloaded Antigravity, and within an hour I had a custom Jekyll theme that was easily customizable and extendable. Antigravity has **supercharged** my **productivity** and **creativity**. 

This is the first time I've seen a proper **Implementation Plan**, **Task List**, and **Walkthrough** by an AI Agent. The ability to influence the **Implementation Plan** with in-line comments before the task executes is **brilliant** and *obvious* at the same time. And the pending comments let me change directions on the fly, saving reverts and churn.

Using this method I was able to pound out feature after feature in a few sessions of watching sports on the couch. Some features I started in **AI Studio** and polished with **opus-4.5** in **Antigravity**. Linking folders using **Workspaces** worked well for porting data and copy to build this site. I mainly used the **Agent Manager** and mostly used one Agent Session at a time.
